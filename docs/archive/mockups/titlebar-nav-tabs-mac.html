Toolbar navigation, Mac style

The strip above the cards becomes a unified toolbar: traffic lights, then a glass group of two pull-down buttons (Goals, Chats), then the chat's title and subtitle the way Mail and Notes title a window. On the trailing edge, one segmented control switches the side column between Library, Evals and Browser, since only one can be open. The rail keeps its icons. Menus are drawn as macOS draws them.

  • Clicking the selected segment closes the side column, so the control works as both a switcher and a toggle, the same as the rail buttons today.
  • The toolbar grows from 28px to 48px so the glass controls have room and the traffic lights sit centred in it, as on a Mac window with a unified toolbar.
  • Shortcuts are drawn with Mac glyphs; on Linux they read Ctrl and Alt. ⌘1–4 already picks the provider, so the panels take ⌥⌘1–3. ⌘N and ⇧⌘G are free in Rocket today; ⌘N rather than ⌘T, which people expect to open a browser tab.
  • Highlight colour is an indigo close to Rocket's accent, standing in for the Mac's accent colour.
