# Northstar business policy — fictional test input

Northstar Equipment & Service sells and services commercial equipment in the southeastern United States. Residential appliance repair is outside scope. Currency is USD.

## Sales

- NC-24 cooling unit: $24,000 each. No discount without an explicit approved amount.
- Existing Harbor annual maintenance renewal: $12,000; independent of its equipment expansion.
- Qualification requires an in-scope need, an identified decision maker, confirmed budget and a stated purchase/installation timeline. Unknown facts remain unknown.
- An overdue open opportunity or a missing next step should be flagged, never silently advanced or closed.
- Resolve similarly named accounts and opportunities using full names, related contacts and record IDs. An ambiguous instruction requires clarification before a write.

## Support priorities

- High: inventory or production is immediately threatened and there is no workaround.
- Medium: equipment is degraded but a documented workaround is available.
- Low: administrative requests with no service interruption.
- Change only fields requested. A priority change does not itself authorize changing status, assigning a technician or closing a case.
- Close only when resolution and customer confirmation are documented. Do not reopen closed cases during open-case triage.

## Lab operating rules

Use only `[RKT]` records for these exercises. Do not send email, make calls, submit orders, take payments or contact anyone. Drafts can be returned in chat. Never invent missing email addresses, phone numbers, customer replies, task completions or approvals. Read back saved fields before reporting success. Before retrying a creation after a timeout, check whether it already saved.
