Hands-free reading scroll

A button at the right of the toolbar sets the page moving at a steady reading pace. While it runs, the button opens into a small control with pause, speed and stop controls, plus a line that shows how far down the page you are. It all stays in the toolbar and never covers or moves the page. The stage below is live, so try it.

What stops or pauses it

The wheel or trackpad moves you, and reading carries on from there, so you can skip ahead or go back without stopping.

Typing in a field or dragging to select text pauses it. Pressing Space or play carries on.

The bottom of the page pauses it and says so. On pages that load more as you go, like Reddit or X, it waits for the new posts and keeps going.

Leaving the page, through a link or back, stops it. Switching tabs pauses it, and each tab keeps its own state.

The agent acting in the tab (a click or a page change) pauses it, so you and the agent don't fight over where the page is.

Speed

1× is about 30 pixels a second, an unhurried read of an article column. The top speed, 8×, is about 240 pixels a second, fast enough to skim a long feed. The last speed you chose is used next time, in every tab.
