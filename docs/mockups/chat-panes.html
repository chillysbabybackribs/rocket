One button, four layoutsAn add chat button (a speech box with a plus) sits in the composer's text line, left, with the text starting to its right. Each click adds a pane that opens as a new chat. The + among the tabs keeps its meaning: a new tab in this pane. In a fourth pane the button is dimmed: four is the most for now.
Every pane is a whole chatIts own tabs, transcript, composer, folder and model. Turns stream into every pane that shows their chat. The pane the cursor is in is the one the rail's history opens into, and its composer edge is the brighter one.
ClosingNo extra control: closing a pane's last tab closes the pane (when there is more than one), and the others take the space in the same order of layouts, so 4 → 3 → 2 → 1.
With the browser openThe chat card is the column it already is; panes share it. Two side by side get narrow there, so the composer's width scales with its pane and the folder pill truncates as it does today.
