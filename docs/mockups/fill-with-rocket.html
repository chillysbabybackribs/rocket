Fill with Rocket

Right-click a text field, or anywhere in a form, and choose Fill with Rocket. The model you last used reads the page, looks things up wherever it needs to, and writes the answer straight into the field. No chat opens and nothing is kept. A small chip by the field shows what it is doing and lets you stop it. When it is done you can undo, have another go, or see what it read. It never presses submit. Try the states, or right-click the reply box.

Esc stops it · Ctrl Z after it fills

What the model gets

The model you last used, named in the menu. A setting to pin a different one can come later.

The page and where you clicked: the tab, the field (or the whole form) by its ref, and its label. It reads whatever else it needs itself.

Every tool a chat turn has: the browser tools (in other tabs too), its own web search, the files and shell in Rocket's folder, and the notes. It can open the docs to check a fact, or run a command to read a version number.

It works in a fresh session with no chat, so it starts with no history. That keeps it fast and your chats clean.

What it never does

  • Press submit, or any button that sends, posts, pays or saves.
  • Touch a field you didn't point at. For a form, that means only the fields in that form.
  • Fill a password or payment field, or one asking for personal details it would have to guess. Those it leaves marked for you.
  • Overwrite what you've typed. If the field has text, that's what it works from (fix, finish, reply to).

The chip

It sits just above the field's top right corner, over the page in a view of its own. That way it covers the field's label, not the page's buttons under the field. For a form it sits above the first field it filled. It follows the field as you scroll.

While it works, it shows the step it's on, taken from the model's own tool calls: reading the thread, opening a source, running a command. Switch tabs and the tab icon keeps a small spinner.

Once it has filled the field, it offers Undo, Another go and the sources it opened. It fades once you type in the field or click away. Nothing is saved, though failures go in the journal.
